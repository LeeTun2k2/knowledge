# Behavior interview tips

**1. Preparation**
- Master the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
- Practice common questions beforehand.

**2. Structuring Answers**
- Keep responses concise, honest, and on-topic.
- Remain positive. Focus on lessons learned and never badmouth past employers.
- Highlight both your individual strengths and your teamwork skills.
- If you lack a specific past experience, explain how you would handle that situation hypothetically.

**3. Interview Interaction**
- Listen carefully. Ask clarifying questions or take a moment to think if needed.
- Ask the interviewer thoughtful questions at the end.
